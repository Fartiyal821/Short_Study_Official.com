Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy

How Short Study safeguards learner data, manages cookies, and enforces global data protection standards.

Last Updated & Effective Date: September 14, 2026

Core Commitment: Short Study ("we", "our", or "the platform") is committed to total privacy transparency. We believe educational materials should be freely accessible without aggressive surveillance or forced account registration. This document details our data handling practices across the website (https://shortstudy.in/), explaining your legal rights under GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), CCPA/CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act), and COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act).

1. Identity of the Data Controller

For the purposes of the General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR and UK GDPR) and other international data privacy legislations, the Data Controller responsible for this website is:

Data Controller: Short Study Educational Initiative
Founder & Administrator: Gaurav Fartiyal
Contact Email: support@shortstudy.in
Location: Uttarakhand, India
Website: https://shortstudy.in/

2. Categories of Information We Collect

Short Study does not mandate user accounts, passwords, or telephone verification to browse our coding tutorials, read academic notes, or take self-assessment quizzes. We collect only the following minimal categories of data:

  • Automated Technical & Usage Logs: When you access our servers, standard server logs automatically record your Internet Protocol (IP) address (truncated or pseudonymized where applicable), user agent string (browser type and version), operating system, language preferences, referring and exit Uniform Resource Locators (URLs), and time-stamped visit metrics.
  • Aggregated Engagement Metrics: We observe aggregate page views, average reading durations per tutorial, scroll depth, and search queries within our notes repository to evaluate which curricula require expansion.
  • Voluntary Correspondence: If you submit an inquiry through our Contact page form or email us directly, we receive the information you intentionally share, including your name, email address, message body, and any supplemental diagnostic attachments.

3. Google AdSense, Advertising Networks & DART Cookies

To sustain our free educational service without charging subscription fees or paywalling study notes, Short Study partners with third-party advertising vendors, primarily Google AdSense.

Google operates as a third-party vendor and uses cookies to serve ads on Short Study. Google's use of advertising cookies—including the DoubleClick DART cookie—enables it and its publishing partners to serve ads to our users based on their prior visits to Short Study and other destinations on the World Wide Web:

  • Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Advertisements: Depending on your geographical region and consent preferences selected through our Consent Management Platform (CMP), ads served may be personalized (tailored to your past browsing history and inferential interests) or non-personalized (contextually aligned only with the academic programming subject of the visited page).
  • Opt-Out of Google DART Cookies: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the official Google Ads Settings Portal.
  • Network Advertising Initiative (NAI): Alternatively, you may opt out of third-party vendor cookie usage across hundreds of ad networks by consulting the Network Advertising Initiative Consumer Opt-Out Page or the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) WebChoices Tool.

4. Web Analytics & Opt-Out Mechanisms

We deploy privacy-conscious third-party analytics technologies (such as Google Analytics) to monitor aggregate traffic trends and diagnose technical bottlenecks. Analytics cookies compile pseudonymous metrics without correlating your IP address with personally identifiable user dossiers.

If you prefer that your browsing interactions on Short Study are not tracked by Google Analytics, you can install the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on, which instructs the Google Analytics JavaScript snippet (ga.js, analytics.js, and gtag.js) to cease relaying visit information to Google Analytics servers.

5. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Compliance

For visitors residing within the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom (UK), or Switzerland, we process personal data in accordance with lawful bases established under GDPR Article 6:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): For the placement of non-essential cookies, personalized advertising identifiers, and detailed analytics tracking, which you explicitly authorize or reject via our CMP consent banner.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): For monitoring server security, repelling Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) attacks, preserving system integrity, and responding to direct learner queries.

Under Chapter III of the GDPR, European residents possess the following guaranteed rights:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): The right to request copies of any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal records.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): The right to request the permanent deletion of your personal communications or email records.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): The right to restrict the manner in which your data is processed under specified conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): The right to receive your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): The right to object at any time to the processing of your data based on legitimate interests.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Controller at support@shortstudy.in. We respond to verified requests within thirty (30) calendar days free of charge.

6. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA) Compliance

This section applies exclusively to residents of the State of California under the California Consumer Privacy Act of 2018, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act of 2020 (CPRA):

  • Right to Know: California consumers may request disclosure of the specific pieces and categories of personal information we have collected, the sources of collection, and the commercial purposes for processing.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of personal information collected directly from you, subject to statutory exemptions.
  • Right to Correct: You have the right to request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, charge differentiated prices, or reduce educational tutorial access because you exercised your statutory privacy rights.

"Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" Statement: Short Study does not sell personal information for monetary compensation, nor do we disclose personal information to data brokers. Under California's broad definition of "sale" or "sharing" involving cross-context behavioral advertising cookies, you may opt out at any time by configuring your cookie choices via our CMP banner or enabling the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal in your browser.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Short Study provides secondary-school curriculum notes (such as CBSE Class 8, 9, and 10 Science and Mathematics). We are deeply sensitive to the safety of underage learners. Our website operates in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA, 15 U.S.C. 6501 et seq.):

  • We do not require children under the age of 13 to register, disclose names, submit birth dates, or submit contact information.
  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • If a parent or legal guardian discovers that their child has sent personal information to us via email or contact forms without parental consent, please contact us immediately at support@shortstudy.in. Upon verification, we will promptly excise such records from our systems.

8. Data Security & Retention

We enforce modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 encryption) across all web traffic to prevent interception. Server access is restricted to verified administrators via multi-factor authentication. Routine server logs are purged every 90 days. Voluntary email inquiries are kept only for the duration required to address the inquiry, after which they are archived or deleted.

9. Revisions to this Privacy Policy

We review and update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect emerging regulatory frameworks and service refinements. When changes occur, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document will be modified. Continued use of Short Study following notice of updates denotes your acceptance of the amended terms.
