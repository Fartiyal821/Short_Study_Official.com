How to reach us — step by step

  1. Choose your channel. Use email for detailed requests (like asking for a specific lesson or reporting a broken link), or Instagram for quick questions and updates.
  2. Mention the topic. If your query is about a lesson, include the language or subject (Python, C, Data Structures, etc.) — this helps us respond faster.
  3. Describe the issue clearly. A short, specific message (for example, "The Python Basics loop example isn't clear") gets resolved much quicker than a general one.
  4. Wait for our reply. We personally read and respond to every message — see our typical response time below.
